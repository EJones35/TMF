Ranks

Novitiate

This rank is given at the choice of your Flight Commander

A rank given to an Archivist when they are just starting out.

Sentinel

This rank is given at the choice of your Flight Commander

A rank given to an Archivist on the path to greatness.

Academician

This rank is given at the choice of your Flight Commander

A rank given to an Archivist who has not just potential, but persuit of knowledge and exploration.

Curator

This rank is given at the choice of your Flight Commander

A rank given to an Archivist who shows leadership to its finest. They have shown the ability to find knowledge and also store it. After all, knowledge is power...

Marshal

This rank is given at the choice of your Flight Commander

A rank given to an Archivist who has shown the sheer amount of knowledge they are willing to collect and skills needed to store it as such.

Archon

This rank is given by the choice of those above with the final decision given by the Aethel.

A rank given to an Archivist who commands a ship full time.

Chancellor

This rank is given by the choice of those above with the final decision given by the Aethel.

A rank given to an Archivist who commands a small fleet of ships full time.

Prelate

This rank is given by the choice of those above with the final decision given by the Aethel.

A rank given to an Archivist who commands a larger fleet of ships than the Chancellor below them.

Paragon

This rank is given by the choice of those above with the final decision given by the Aethel.

A rank given to an Archivist who is a Zenith's right hand.

Zenith

This rank is given by the choice of those above with the final decision given by the Aethel.

A rank given to an Archivist who is the highest, non-birthright role. Some notable examples are Zeniths Nila and Lowell.

Aethel

This rank is given by blood relation

The only rank inaccessible to anyone not blood related to the current Aethel. A very rare figure to meet. If you do, it'll either be something really good, or really bad...
